# Responsible use

hexvero analyzes software statically on your own machine. Use it only on software you own, that is
open source, or that you are explicitly authorized to examine. Respect the licenses and terms of the
software you inspect and of any analysis engine you connect (radare2, rizin and Ghidra are open
source; commercial tools need a valid license).

hexvero does not break protections, decrypt content, bypass licensing or modify targets. Findings
describe what the bytes contain; interpretation and any further action are your responsibility.
